# Competitor Matrix — Personal AI Call Assistant

**Status:** Desk-research snapshot; not a final market conclusion. **Checked:** 2026-10-11. Claims and store metadata can change. No purchase, hands-on benchmark, or independent product test has been completed.

## Executive take
The category is validated by competitors, especially Equal AI and hiRobin. A generic “AI answers unknown calls and sends a summary” product is not differentiated enough. Discovery must establish whether we can deliver a more reliable/transparent call-routing experience, a narrow initial segment, or trustworthy domain actions without excessive privacy and operational risk.

## Differentiation hypotheses to validate
1. **Trust and control:** visible routing status, clear AI disclosure, simple takeover/disable, explainable call decisions.
2. **Local-language quality:** code-switching and noisy-call performance measured with consented scripts.
3. **Useful outcomes:** actionable structured summaries, not merely transcripts.
4. **Profile portability:** backend-managed profiles with explicit, audited capabilities; no arbitrary user-supplied tools.
5. **Hospitality utility:** an operator profile can answer approved FAQs and capture intent through a trusted Antena API; it must not invent availability, prices, reservations or policy exceptions.
6. **Data minimisation:** transparent audio/transcript retention and deletion status.

## Pricing evidence caveat
Equal AI's official terms state that paid Android features may be offered through one-time unlocks or subscriptions and that prices/features can vary by user, offer and purchase point. The App Store listing exposes ₹149 and ₹249 in-app purchase entries, but these should not be treated as a universal subscription price. Source: https://myequal.ai/terms-and-conditions/ and https://apps.apple.com/in/app/equal-ai-call-assistant/id6757906431.

## Unknowns
- Competitor call completion rate, takeover latency, language error rate, disclosure behavior and end-to-end cost.
- Third-party app reliability on target SIM/carrier/device.
- Willingness to pay beyond native screening/spam protection.
- Whether hospitality should be a consumer profile or separate B2B offering.

## Next actions
Run a scripted, same-device comparison against Equal AI, hiRobin and Truecaller where technically and legally possible. Record device, carrier, route, call outcome, latency, language, disclosure, takeover, controls and observed cost. Do not record real third-party calls without a reviewed lawful consent process.
